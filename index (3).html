Sign and speech, understood both ways.

SignCare AI helps people who are deaf or have a speech impairment talk with people who do not know Indian Sign Language. It reads signs from a webcam, speaks them aloud, and turns spoken words back into text.

Open the tools See how it works

Hackathon build. Sign recognition runs in the Python backend and needs a model you train yourself (see README).

What it does

Six features, built in order. Each one says whether it needs a trained model.

Sign recognition

needs model

The webcam finds your hand with MediaPipe. A Keras model trained on 15–20 ISL signs turns the hand movement into a word.

Speech output

no model

Recognised words are joined into a sentence and read aloud with text-to-speech.

Two-way talk

no model

A hearing person speaks. Their words appear as text, with a sign clip shown for each word that has a verified clip.

Uncertainty check

needs model

When the model is not sure, it shows its top guesses and asks you to pick the right one. Your choice is saved as a new example.

Personal signs

needs model

Record your own version of a sign. The app stores the examples in SQLite and retrains so it learns your style.

Emergency help

rules + confirm

Emergency signs show a large message. An alert goes to your chosen contact only after you confirm.

How a sign becomes a sentence

WebcamOpenCV reads frames.
Hand trackingMediaPipe gives 21 points per hand.
Sequence30 frames of points are stacked.
LSTM modelKeras predicts the sign and a confidence score.
CheckLow confidence asks the user to confirm.
OutputText on screen and spoken audio.

Streamlit shows the interface. SQLite stores confirmed corrections, personal signs and emergency contacts.

Starting vocabulary

Begin with these 18 everyday and emergency words. Before you record or train, check each sign against the ISLRTC Indian Sign Language dictionary. Do not use a sign you have not checked.

Roadmap and limits

Stage 1

Set up Python, camera and MediaPipe. Draw hand points live.

Stage 2

Collect data for 18 signs and save landmark sequences.

Stage 3

Train the LSTM, check accuracy on people not in the training data.

Stage 4

Add speech, two-way mode, uncertainty prompts, personal signs, emergency alert.

Limits

Small vocabulary, single signs rather than full sentences, sensitive to light and camera angle, and not a replacement for a human interpreter. Similar projects exist, so present this as your own build, not a first.
